01 — The Opportunity

National Harbor is a destination. It should also be a digital brand.

Your tenants have foot traffic, physical presence, and loyal customers — but most of them are leaving significant revenue on the table because they don't have the infrastructure or expertise to sell online at scale. That gap is the opportunity.

What tenants have
  • Physical retail presence and brand recognition
  • Existing customer base and repeat foot traffic
  • Real products that photograph and film well
  • A location that draws creators and tourists naturally
What most tenants lack
  • Active TikTok Shop listings and creator programs
  • Meta ads strategy and budget allocation
  • Amazon marketplace presence (or optimization)
  • A dedicated team to manage digital operations
💡
The insight: The creator economy is democratizing retail — brands that sell on TikTok Shop alongside Meta and Amazon are seeing 3–10× the revenue of those that don't. National Harbor can be the first destination retail complex in the country to offer its tenants a unified digital commerce program.

03 — Quarterly Creator Meetups

Bringing creators to National Harbor. Four times a year.

The physical location is a competitive advantage most digital agencies can't offer. Cult Content organizes quarterly creator events at National Harbor — bringing social media creators directly to participating tenants for live content creation, brand relationships, and authentic UGC at scale.

Content creation inside the destination.

Creators visit National Harbor, shoot content in the actual stores and venues, and post it to their audiences. Tenants get authentic, high-performing content. National Harbor gets organic reach and foot traffic from the creator's followers. Everyone wins.

Before the event
Creator Selection
CC curates and invites 20–40 creators matched to participating tenant categories — beauty, food, retail, lifestyle.
Day of
Live Content Creation
Creators visit each tenant, receive product, and create content on-site. CC provides briefs and on-the-ground coordination.
After the event
Distribution & Tracking
Content goes live across TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube. CC tracks GMV, views, and engagement driven back to each tenant.
What Cult Content provides
  • Creator sourcing, vetting, and invitations
