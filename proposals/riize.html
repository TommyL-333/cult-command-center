04 — Month 1 Strategy
Investment Month — Build the Foundation

Month 1 is designed to run at a loss. That's the plan — not a warning. We front-load the algorithm signal, creator relationships, and social proof so that every subsequent month converts at a fundamentally different rate.

The Rebate Campaign — Month 1 Only
We run a rebate campaign in the first month to generate real order volume immediately. This feeds the TikTok algorithm a strong sales signal before organic content ramps up — and seeds verified reviews that make every subsequent video convert better. The rebate budget is a one-time cost in Month 1 and does not recur in future months.
