04 — Month 1 Strategy
Investment Month — Build the Foundation

Month 1 is designed to go negative. That's not a bug — it's the strategy. We front-load the algorithm signal, the creator relationships, and the social proof, so that Month 2 and beyond convert at a completely different rate.

The Rebate Campaign — Month 1
We run a rebate campaign in the first month to generate real order volume immediately. This feeds the TikTok algorithm a strong sales signal before organic content fully kicks in — and seeds reviews that make every subsequent video convert better. This is not a shortcut. It's how you build a shop that creators actually want to post about.
Step 1
Real customers purchase Riize on TikTok Shop — generating immediate GMV and order history
Step 2
Customers receive a rebate after leaving a verified review — building social proof fast
Step 3
Algorithm registers sales velocity → creator outreach tiers unlock faster → organic flywheel starts
⚠️
Month 1 will show a net loss. The rebate campaign costs money upfront. Combined with the management fee, creator retainers, and sampling budget, total spend will exceed GMV in month one. This is expected, planned, and necessary. Brands that skip this step spend months waiting for organic traction that the rebate campaign delivers in weeks.
