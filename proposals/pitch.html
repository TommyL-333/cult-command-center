05 — Business Model

A model that compounds.

Two revenue streams. One scales automatically as clients grow. The other grows as we add clients. Both are generating revenue today.

Management Fee
Fixed monthly retainer per brand client. Covers strategy, creator management, campaign operations, and reporting. Predictable, recurring baseline revenue that scales with the client roster.
Net Sales Share
A percentage of net sales generated through the affiliate channel. As brand GMV grows, revenue grows automatically — without adding headcount. This is the compounding engine.
