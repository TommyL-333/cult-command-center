Growth Strategy

Quality first. Volume second. Ads last.

Most brands try to throw ad spend at a product before they have enough content to make ads convert. We don't do that. We build the creative library first, then activate paid distribution once we know what's working.

Phase 1 — Ignition (Months 1–2)
  • Get TikTok FBT shipment in, shop fully configured and live
  • Hydration Refreshers as the lead SKU — one focused message
  • Seed 100–150 units/month to hand-selected creators aligned with the product
  • $100 creator bonus for meeting the 10-video brief threshold — minimum viable incentive
  • One retainer creator locked in to set the creative tone and establish social proof
  • No ad spend until creative volume supports it
Phase 2 — Content-to-Commerce (Months 2–3)
  • Open Collab affiliate program goes live — 15% commission, broad creator pool
  • Expand to targeted outreach at 25–30% commission for proven TikTok Shop performers
  • Evaluate CTR, engagement, and conversion data from Phase 1 creative
  • Drop Energy line once Hydration has social proof and reorder runway
  • GMV Max ads activated only after enough creatives exist to feed the algorithm
  • GMV Max budget: ~10% of net sales, not a fixed spend
⚡

Founder content is not optional. Nobody explains your product better than you do. A few videos of Kamel making it — the origin story, why you built it, the ritual behind it — give creators something to reference and give the brand a face. You don't need to go viral. You need to exist on the platform.

📦

Shopify website — prioritize this. TikTok and Amazon are your launch channels, but brands without a website look unfinished to creators who are vetting whether to promote you. A simple Shopify store builds legitimacy, creates a direct channel for customer data, and opens up future paid traffic options. We recommend getting this live in parallel with your TikTok Shop launch.
