TikTok rewards momentum

Every order through TikTok Shop builds algorithm trust — the platform surfaces products with sales history more aggressively than new listings. Brands that start fast own the feed. Brands that start slow spend 4–6 months catching up.

Alpha Flow's organic CTR already tells the algorithm this product belongs in more feeds. Giving it creator volume makes that signal undeniable.

Creator stacking compounds without proportional cost

Month 1: 30 creators, 170 videos. Month 2: those 30 post again — now with product familiarity and proven hooks — plus new creators layer on top. Active content count grows every month, reach expands, and cost per view falls.

C2 is also a talent pipeline. The creators who break out get promoted to C1 agreements — the system identifies its own stars and self-optimizes over time.

🔁
The Amazon flywheel is real. TikTok content drives brand name searches on Amazon. UGC and creator reviews reduce Amazon's ad cost. Amazon profitability funds next month's creator budget. TikTok and Amazon aren't separate channels — they're one engine. The brands that treat them that way win both.
