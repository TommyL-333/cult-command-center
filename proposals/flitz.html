01 — Strategy
Two-Phase Launch Plan

Since Christmas isn't your peak season, we don't want to throw ad dollars at the wall during Q4. Instead we use the next few months to lay the groundwork — so when January hits and creators are starving for a new deal, Flitz is already dialed in.

Phase 1 — Now

Soft Launch & Data Gathering

Get TikTok Shop live. List hero SKU. Run a small Join Brands campaign. Bring Crystal and a handful of early affiliates into the creator community. Begin collecting conversion rates, click-through rates, and video performance data so we go into January armed with real metrics — not guesses.

Phase 2 — January 2027

Full Activation

Scale creator outreach to thousands per week. Run bonuses and incentives (post 10 videos → $100, 5 sales → $300 bonus). Activate the full Cult Members community. Push promotional calendar and flash sales. This is where the TikTok Machine goes to work — and your inventory forecasting has a real baseline to plan around.

📈
Why this timing works: After the holidays, creators are actively looking for new brand deals. Your product has a built-in "wow moment" that performs exceptionally well in short-form video — the goal is to have your creator community, conversion data, and shop performance score established before we turn up the volume.
🧲
Crystal opportunity: The creator who drove your 12M-view Instagram viral moment has already expressed interest in becoming an affiliate. We'll bring her into the community immediately, get her set up on TikTok Shop, and use her story — $50,000 in sales from one video — to inspire and recruit other creators on our weekly calls.
02 — Creator Program
Three Tiers Working Together

The brands that win on TikTok Shop don't rely on random viral spikes — they build creator community. When a video goes viral, we want 100 creators replicating it, not waiting to see if it happens again.

Join Brands Campaign
Launch Mechanism
  • 300 creators receive free product + small upfront fee to create their first video
  • Each creator places an order — generating initial GMV to unlock higher outreach tiers
  • Produces immediate creator-generated content you can repurpose for Meta ads
  • One-time activation, not an ongoing monthly spend
Retainer Creators
Paid, Guaranteed Content
  • Screened through our network — selected for product fit and engagement quality
  • Paid $500/creator for a guaranteed 20 videos per month
  • Dedicated to Flitz; produce consistent, on-brand content each month
  • First priority for product testing and new SKU launches
Cult Members
Community — 700+ Network
  • Free product samples in exchange for the opportunity to earn commission on sales
  • Boosted affiliate commission links — higher earning potential than standard program
  • Brand-specific training: what's working, what angles drive conversions, viral replications
  • No guaranteed content output — they post because it works for them
  • Some Cult Members outperform retainer creators; we watch the data and reward the top performers
📞
Weekly Creator Call: Every week, we host a live call with Flitz's full creator community. Brands jump on. Creators share what's working. We review viral content and coordinate replications. We surface the top performers on a leaderboard and spotlight wins (like Crystal's $50k moment) to keep the community bought in. This community infrastructure is what separates brands that get one spike from brands that get a hundred of them.
