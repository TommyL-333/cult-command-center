03 · Live Strategy

A Full Live Program — Not Just Going Live.

Most brands treat TikTok LIVE as an afterthought. This live management package builds a real program around your best products: a product audit across your catalog, a full live strategy, trained creators, and coordinated flash sales designed to generate order velocity.

🎬
Led by Becca Bryan. Becca brings her own creator network to the table — live-experienced talent who know how to structure sessions, handle flash promos, and keep viewer engagement high. This isn't outsourced production; it's a built-in strategic advantage.
🔍
Product Audit
We review your brand's full catalog and identify which SKUs have the best live potential — strongest margin, most visual appeal, easiest to demonstrate and convert on camera.
📋
Full Live Strategy
We build the live playbook: session structure, talking points, product framing, offer timing, and what makes viewers convert. Delivered as a repeatable format your creators can run.
⚡
Flash Sale Setup
We design and schedule flash sales timed to live sessions — limited-time pricing, giveaway mechanics, and bundle offers that drive urgency and order velocity during each broadcast.
🎓
Creator Live Training
Becca runs brand-specific live training with your creators — scripts, session flow, handling the chat, and converting viewers into buyers. Creators come ready, not improvising.
📊
Weekly Creator Performance Call — 30 minutes
Once a week we run a structured 30-minute call with your creators: who's on the leaderboard this week, who's posted the most videos, who's driving the highest GMV, and what we need from them going into the next week. Creators know exactly where they stand and what to focus on — which keeps content consistent and quality high.
🔥
Why creators go live without being paid to: Once affiliates understand the flash sale structure and see how much revenue a live session can generate, there is no hesitation. We've seen it every time — creators who were nervous about going live jump in with both feet once they know how to prep and know what's in it for them. The training, the strategy, and the flash sales are what make that happen.
➕
Optional add-on — Guaranteed Live Sessions: Want specific creators confirmed to go live on a set schedule? Guaranteed live sessions are available at $40/hour per creator — billed against actual live time, managed by Becca's network.
