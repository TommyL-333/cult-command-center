The Core Problem

23,000 affiliates on commission-only is not a creator strategy.

The Torriden affiliate data confirms what we see across every commission-only TAP campaign: a long tail of inactive creators, a handful of self-motivated performers, and no mechanism to direct who posts, when, or what. The fix isn't more creators — it's better compensation for fewer, better-briefed ones.

Commission-Only (Current State)
  • 23K creators linked — most have never posted
  • Average creator generates $4.60/month in Torriden GMV
  • No mechanism to enforce brief compliance
  • Top performers self-select but can't be directed
  • BFCM window relies on creators deciding on their own to post
Fixed Rate + Commission (Proposed)
  • 3–5 top creators on fixed rates — they're locked in and briefed
  • Brief compliance is enforceable — there's a financial arrangement
  • Content cadence and messaging are controlled
  • BFCM push is coordinated, not accidental
  • Commission layer keeps conversion incentive active after the fee
